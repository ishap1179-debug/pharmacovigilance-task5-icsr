# pharmacovigilance-task5-icsr
Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) – ADR identification, reporting, causality assessment and classification.

### Case Study

A 28-year-old female patient was prescribed **Amoxicillin 500 mg orally three times daily** for a bacterial throat infection. On the **3rd day of treatment**, the patient developed a **generalized red skin rash with itching** over the trunk and arms.

The patient stopped taking amoxicillin after consulting the physician. The symptoms gradually improved and the patient recovered within a few days without hospitalization.

---

## 01. ADR IDENTIFICATION

### Patient Information
- Age: 28 years
- Sex: Female
- Medical condition: Bacterial throat infection
- Patient outcome: Recovered

### Suspected Medicine
- Drug: Amoxicillin
- Dose: 500 mg
- Route: Oral
- Frequency: Three times daily
- Indication: Bacterial throat infection

### Adverse Drug Reaction
- Generalized skin rash
- Itching (pruritus)

### Onset
The adverse reaction appeared on Day 3 after starting amoxicillin.

---

## 02. REPORTING (ICSR)

### Report Type
- Type: Spontaneous ADR report
- Source: Healthcare professional

### Suspected Drug
- Name: Amoxicillin
- Dose: 500 mg
- Route: Oral
- Frequency: Three times daily
- Indication: Bacterial throat infection

### Adverse Event
The patient developed a generalized red skin rash accompanied by itching.

### Action Taken
Amoxicillin was discontinued after the adverse reaction was reported.

### Outcome
The patient recovered after discontinuation of the suspected medicine.

### ICSR Summary
A 28-year-old female developed generalized rash and itching on Day 3 of amoxicillin therapy. The suspected medicine was discontinued and the patient recovered within a few days.

---

## 03. CAUSALITY ASSESSMENT

### Causality: PROBABLE

### Reasoning
- The reaction occurred after starting amoxicillin.
- The onset was temporally compatible with drug exposure.
- Skin rash and itching are known reactions associated with amoxicillin.
- The patient improved after withdrawal of the suspected medicine.
- No other significant cause was reported.

Therefore, the relationship between amoxicillin and the adverse reaction is assessed as **Probable**.

---

## 04. CLASSIFICATION

| Parameter | Classification |
|---|---|
| Seriousness | Non-serious |
| Severity | Moderate |
| Expectedness | Expected |
| Causality | Probable |
| Outcome | Recovered |
| Hospitalization | No |

### Final Assessment

The case represents a **non-serious, moderate, expected adverse drug reaction** associated with amoxicillin. Based on the temporal relationship, known reaction profile, and improvement after withdrawal, the causality is assessed as **Probable**.
